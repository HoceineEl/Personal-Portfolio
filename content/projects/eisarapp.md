## EisarApp – Multi-Tenant Training SaaS

### Overview

[Eisar](https://eisarapp.sa?ref=portfolio) is a cloud platform that lets companies in Saudi Arabia run their training and development department from one place. Each company gets its own space: it adds the employees who need training, builds training paths for them, runs the programs, and follows the results in reports.

It's in Arabic, with offices in Riyadh and Makkah, and offers dedicated paths for sectors such as hospitality. I worked on it as a full stack developer, built for NewTags.

![The Eisar website: run your training and development department from one place](/images/my_projects/eisarapp/cover.webp)

### What the platform does

- **Multi-tenant**: each company has its own separate training environment
- **Training paths**: design learning paths and assign them to the right employees
- **Automated programs**: training programs and training forms that run on their own
- **Certificates**: design and issue training certificates
- **Reports**: progress dashboards and customizable reports on where training stands

![Eisar's features: design training paths and build employees' skills](/images/my_projects/eisarapp/features.webp)

### Stack

- **Laravel** for the backend and multi-tenancy
- **Livewire** for the interactive parts
- **FilamentPHP** for the dashboards
- **Tailwind CSS** for the interface
